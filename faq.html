Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total maximum supply cap for Parallax (PLX)?

The Parallax (PLX) blockchain enforces a strict, protocol-level hard supply cap of 84,000,000 (84 million) total coins. New coins enter circulation strictly through sustainable Scrypt Proof-of-Work mining consensus.

What is the current block reward for mining Parallax (PLX)?

The current block subsidy is 50 PLX per block. This reward is issued directly on the native Layer 1 blockchain layer to miners who successfully contribute computing power to secure and validate network transactions.

Is Parallax (PLX) an independent coin or a token?

Parallax (PLX) is a native Layer 1 cryptocurrency coin running on its own independent Scrypt Proof-of-Work blockchain network. It is not an ERC-20, BEP-20, or smart-contract utility token hosted on an external ecosystem.
